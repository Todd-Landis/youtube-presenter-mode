Background playback and sync

Keep presenter mode running while you use other apps, ideally for a full-length video. Gaps are state messages more than 1.5 s apart; stalls are stretches where the video advanced less than half as fast as the clock.

Command delivery

How remote-bar commands reached the player. Commands that can't be sent directly are queued and picked up with the player's next state report (within about half a second).
