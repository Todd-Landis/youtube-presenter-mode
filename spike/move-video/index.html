Move a playing video into another window

Presenter Mode's experimental move mode opens a same-origin popup and moves the page's own <video> into it, instead of reopening the page on the TV. This page checks what that relies on, in the browser you open it in: whether the element keeps playing (a plain MP4, and MediaSource fed by this page's script), whether it shows a picture, whether this page can still control it, and what happens when this page is hidden, reloaded or closed.
